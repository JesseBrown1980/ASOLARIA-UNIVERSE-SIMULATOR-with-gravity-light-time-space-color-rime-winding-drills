### 2. The free/translucent denominator and the additive rainbow

Every reduction or population statement must therefore report:

1. the total denominator;

### 3. Folder, watcher, reader, dispatcher: a phased voice

missing separations explicit:

```text
folder/room -> watcher -> reader -> dispatcher -> executor -> receipt -> collector
catalog/body   observe     resolve    authorize     act         bind        return waste
```

- A **folder/room** can be a catalog address or an executable container.
- A **watcher** detects a bounded state or trigger.
- A **reader** resolves the address, language, tuple, context, and intended body.
- A **dispatcher** binds the verb to an allowed executor and authority scope.
- The **executor** acts only after the gate; a catalog entry grants no authority.

past, present, or future positions around a spherical route. No one phase is “the
speaker,” and the historical Node executor is not the current architecture by default.


### 5. Frozen/playable crossed with spherical past/present/future

State and temporal address are orthogonal:

| state | past | present | future |
|---|---|---|---|
| **frozen** | recorded slice or receipt | stopped current slice | indexed later position, declared route, or precomputed slice |
| **playable** | replay a retained sequence | execute the current bounded step | traverse a later position already represented in the played series |

A causal live stream sees only what has arrived. A whole retained/played series can
apply a centered past-present-future operator because all three indexed positions are
available. “Future addressable” here does **not** mean prophecy or external time travel;
it means a later coordinate exists in the selected series or route.

Likewise, spherical directions and axis permutations are views around a common center,
not independent beings. Every receipt must retain subject, seat, transform, time index,
and ownership rule. Frozen/playable state says whether the slice can be stepped;
past/present/future says where the reader points.

### 6. The screen is the return path, not the substrate

Several dated roots combine in the screen path: ID 149's external videotape, ID 244's
frame renderer, ID 255's projected path, IDs 363/367's 2D slices, and ID 384's RGB/light
language. The joined path is `LIRIS_ANALYSIS`:

```text
world/corpus
  -> capture or ingest
  -> frozen slice + hash
  -> catalog/address
  -> watcher + reader
  -> authorized dispatcher + executor/play
```

The screen is where an internal representation returns to visible emitted light. It is

camera pointed at the display creates a new observation layer, potentially including
the screen's own pixel grid, exposure, and color transform. That returned slice must be
indexed separately from the source and from the process that rendered it.
