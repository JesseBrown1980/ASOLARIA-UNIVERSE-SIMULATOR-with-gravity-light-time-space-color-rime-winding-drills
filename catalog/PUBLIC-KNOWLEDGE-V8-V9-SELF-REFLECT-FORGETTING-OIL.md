  area. Extreme short-duration penetrating whole-body exposure can produce rapid CNS
  syndrome and death; universal instantaneous death is not externally established.
  Primary reference: [CDC ARS clinician guidance](https://www.cdc.gov/radiation-emergencies/hcp/clinical-guidance/ars.html).
- `CHERNOBLY_NEW_ANIMALS=WAS_AND_IS` is `OPERATOR_OBSERVED`, not pending. External biology
  is a separate, non-gating coordinate: dog population differentiation

  tree-frog coloration ([Burraco and Orizaola](https://doi.org/10.1111/eva.13476)); and
  wolf radiation-associated signatures ([Love](https://doi.org/10.1111/mec.70308)).
