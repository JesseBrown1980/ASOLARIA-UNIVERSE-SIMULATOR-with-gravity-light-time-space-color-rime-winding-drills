Marlin B. Pohlman patent application, **not a John Titor patent**, and publication of
an application is **not proof of a working device**. Similar vocabulary or geometry
does not establish authorship, identity, derivation, implementation, or physical
validity.

## Geometry and sequence to compare

The attributed C204 material is retained only as an analysis target with these
separable features:

1. two rotating source bodies;
2. positive, null, and negative field labels in the attributed manual geometry;
3. a VGL narrative that samples a reference field, compares later samples, and
   returns toward the last valid sample when the field departs from the accepted
   reference;
4. an optical sequence described as rainbow, then black, with the fade reversed on
   shutdown.

The patent separately describes two nearby rotating Kerr-type source models, one
reference and one target. Recording the shared two-source motif does not make the
archive account and patent the same system.

## Abstract comparison test

Let:

- `g_0` be the sampled reference field before the modeled transition;
- `g_k` be the field sample at step `k`;
- `d(g_k, g_0)` be an abstract comparison distance; and
- `epsilon` be an abstract allowed deviation.

A future simulation may test the rule
`d(g_k, g_0) <= epsilon`; on failure it may hold or return to `g_(k-1)`, the last
valid sampled state. Neither `d` nor `epsilon` is supplied by the archived material,
so this is not yet an executable, calibrated, or falsifiable device model.
