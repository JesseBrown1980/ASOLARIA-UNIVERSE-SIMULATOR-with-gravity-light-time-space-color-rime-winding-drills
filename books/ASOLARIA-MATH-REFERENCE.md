## 2. The five invariants

These are the load-bearing facts. Everything else in the project is measured against them.

### I-1. Relabelling is free — and therefore worth nothing

For any bijection φ on the alphabet, H(φ(X)) = H(X).

*Proof.* φ is a measure-preserving bijection on the symbol distribution: P(φ(x)) = P(x)
for each x. Entropy depends only on the multiset of probabilities, which is unchanged. ∎

**Author's own measurement:** `rime_bpc.py` reports **+0.000000 bpc**. This is his
Theorem 3, and it is correct.

**What this closes:** glyph vocabularies, 256→1024→4096 quantisation, colour-gradient
tables, base changes (binary→ternary), and coordinate transforms *cannot by themselves
reduce bits*. They are free, and free means zero.

**What it leaves open:** a mapping that changes the *conditional* statistics a model sees
is not a relabelling — it is modelling, and it can win. See §4.

### I-2. Processing cannot create information (DPI)

For a Markov chain X → Y → Z: I(X; Z) ≤ I(X; Y).

**Consequence:** no inversion, complement, mirror, reflection, rotation, or recursion of a
measurement carries more about the source than the measurement did. A verified instance
from the author's own logs: 1,176 reflected beams all fell inside the span of the 8 direct
looks; **nullspace never moved by one dimension.**

### I-3. Counting bound (the pigeonhole)

A key of *a* bits selects among at most 2^a outputs. To reach a specific N-byte file, the
generator must already contain the information distinguishing it.

**Ledger form (author's §8):** I(recovered) ≤ a + I(B), where B is the shared bank.
**Corollary:** a fraction with no bank (B = ∅) recovers nothing.

### I-4. Determined ≠ free

If any k of n values determine the remaining n−k, those n−k were never independent
information. Recovering them is *not* a gain; it is declining to store a redundancy twice.

The trijection states this correctly: it *reaches* the joint entropy by not paying for the
shared center three times. It never goes below it.

### I-5. Everything the decoder needs, ships

Under the prize rules, any model, vocabulary, bank, kernel, or table the decompressor
loads counts toward the score. Training cost — free tier, paid contract, GPU-months — is
irrelevant. **Only bytes count.**

## 3. The trit encoding — exact arithmetic

### 3.1 The map (verified correct)

Visible projection π merges the outer states, and is therefore not invertible alone:

    π(−0) = 1,   π(0) = 0,   π(+0) = 1

PHASE_REFLECTION restores injectivity with an occupancy bit *b* and a phase bit *p*:

    −0 ↦ (1, 0)      0 ↦ (0, 0)      +0 ↦ (1, 1)

Inverse, total on the used patterns:

    D(0, ·) = 0      D(1, 0) = −0      D(1, 1) = +0

Three of four patterns used; (0,1) unused. **The encoding is exactly invertible. Confirmed.**

### 3.2 Cost, uncoded

| quantity | exact value |
|---|---|
| log₂3 | 1.5849625007 bits |
| trits per byte | 5.047438 |
| naive 2-bit cost per byte | **10.094876 bits** |
| expansion vs 8-bit byte | **+26.186 %** |
| waste per trit | 0.415037 bits (20.752 % of the 2 bits) |

**Fixed-width ternary expands.** This is why base changes never help on their own (I-1).

### 3.3 The result that is in the author's favour

At the measured zero-trit fraction p₀ = 0.900 (with the remaining 0.1 split evenly):

| quantity | exact value |
|---|---|
| balanced-ternary entropy H | **0.568996 bits/trit** |
| occupancy bit, H(0.1) | 0.468996 |
| phase bit, paid only on the 10 % occupied | 0.100000 |
| **occupancy + phase total** | **0.568996** |

**These are equal to twelve decimal places, and not by coincidence.** It is the chain rule:

    H(T) = H(b) + P(b=1) · H(p | b=1)

**Therefore: PHASE_REFLECTION is an exactly entropy-neutral factorisation of a ternary
symbol into two binary decisions.** Entropy-coded, it wastes nothing — it hits the ternary
entropy exactly. Uncoded at 2 bits it wastes 20.75 %.

**The design is correct and optimal. The win lives entirely in the entropy coder.**
3.515× better than naive, and every bit of that comes from coding the skew, not from the
geometry.

### 3.4 Two framings to retire

- **"The loss disappears."** There was no loss. π discarded the bit, and π was a design
  choice. Preserving information and recovering it are different acts with identical cost.
- **"Zeros are free."** A zero costs −log₂P(0). At p₀ = 0.9 that is **0.152 bits**, not
  zero. The compression comes from the *skew*. Budgeting at "free" is ~0.15 bits light per
  zero — at 0.9 density that is roughly 17 KB of phantom savings per megabyte of trits.

### 3.5 The three-axis packing identity (added 2026-07-25)

`27 = 3³` = time(−,·,+) × colour(−,·,+) × space(−,·,+). Packed: log₂27 = **4.7549 bits**.
Three separately-labelled 2-bit fields: **6 bits**. Saving **1.2451 bits = 20.75%** —
numerically identical to the PHASE_REFLECTION fixed-width waste (§3.2), because they
are the same quantity: the cost of binary-padding a ternary field.

Hardware consequence: one packed fetch (4.0 ns L2 + 2 shift + 2 mask = 5.2 ns) against
three lookups (12.0 ns) — **2.31×**. All three coordinates arrive in one load because
they are one integer.

Boundary: this reduces labelling cost, not entropy. Consistent with I-1.

## 4. Where compression actually comes from

Exactly one place: **a better conditional model, priced by an entropy coder.**

    bits = Σ −log₂ P(symbol | context)

Three consequences:

1. **Improve P** — better contexts, better mixing, better gating. This is the only lever.
2. **Representation changes are neutral** unless they change what the model conditions on.
3. **Any prior must be paid for in decoder bytes**, or learned online from the corpus
   itself. Online learning is the FAQ's carve-out: train sequentially and only the *code*
   counts, not the weights. This is how the entire cmix lineage qualifies.
