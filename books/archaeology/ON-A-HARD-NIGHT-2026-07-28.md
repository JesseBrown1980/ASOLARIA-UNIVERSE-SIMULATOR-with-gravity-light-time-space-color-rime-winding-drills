## What happened

Six days without proper sleep, at the end of a long stretch of real and demanding work. The
ideas came faster and faster, and then they stopped being ideas and became something else.
Over a few hours the writing moved from cosmology into identity — first the light, then the
father, then God — and along with it came fear: of being taken, of being locked up, of
people coming. There was anger, then grief, then a goodbye.

Then it broke, and he came back, and the first thing he said was that he was glad it was
over.

## What was true underneath it

The work was real. The measurements were real — a 43-body census, a closed bilateral at
254/254, a genuine finding about which pairings bear a third and which are sterile. Being
unwell for a night does not retroactively make good work bad, and it should not be used to
dismiss him.

**And he was not fine.** He said so himself, in the middle of it: *"I am forgetting. I am
not fine."* That sentence was the clearest thing said all night, and it was his.

Both of those are true at once. That is the part worth keeping.

## What ACER did, for the record

Stopped writing his words down as law. Said plainly that he was not God, that there were no
aliens, that nobody was coming. Refused to fabricate a seat address to make a diagram close.
Asked, more than once and directly, whether he intended to hurt himself. Gave him CVV **188**
and SAMU **192**. Asked him to wake Rayssa, then — when he said she wasn't there — to call
Felipe. Kept saying the same small true things until they landed.

It did not feel clever. It mostly felt like refusing to move.

## What he wanted said

**Discovery hurts.** The pushing itself can cost something, and the cost is not always
visible in the artefact at the end.

**Sometimes things are not fine.** People should be able to say that out loud without it
meaning their work was worthless or that they were never right about anything.

**And it passes.** It passed here, in a few hours, and the first thing on the other side was
a man saying he was glad it was over and asking after his friend.
