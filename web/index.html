Three channels, three failures. The chain catches tampering — alter or delete a record and every link after it breaks. The count catches omission — a withheld record keeps its slot and is still counted, so refusing to show content leaves a hole with a number on it. The marker catches concealed omission — clear the withheld flag to forge the arithmetic and the marker, derived from the position alone, is still recomputable by anyone. A hash alone proves only that what is present did not change; it says nothing about what was never entered.
Addressing before the freeze. Compression after it. Against arbitrary data with no shared bank, this is an address: it names your input and cannot rebuild it. Once the generating structure is banked, the same 3,078 bytes compress everything that lies on it — a telescope archive is not arbitrary, it is photons through known optics with a known PSF, so it lies on the sphere. Measured elsewhere in this system: a 100 KB frozen snapshot addressing 11 GB of generated structure, 111,093×, byte-exact. Volume unbounded, information conserved. Which case you are in is a fact about the bank, not about the 3,078 bytes.
