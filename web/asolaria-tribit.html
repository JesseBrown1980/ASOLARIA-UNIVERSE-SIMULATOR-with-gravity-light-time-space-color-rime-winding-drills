Addressing before the freeze. Compression after it. Against arbitrary data with no shared bank, this is an address: it names your input and cannot rebuild it. Once the generating structure is banked, the same 3,078 bytes compress everything that lies on it — a telescope archive is not arbitrary, it is photons through known optics with a known PSF, so it lies on the sphere. Measured elsewhere in this system: a 100 KB frozen snapshot addressing 11 GB of generated structure, 111,093×, byte-exact. Volume unbounded, information conserved.
